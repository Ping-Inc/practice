[new: public mode] 
this build introduces a new "public mode."

public mode is designed for you to feel safe handing your phone over to a friend to browse select pings.

[new: zoom] 
turning the dial between focus and grid now zooms in and out, instead of switching instantly.

[instructions: public mode]
1) find a ping older than 24 hours that you'd like to make public
2) tap the "public" button that should now be visible
3) enter public mode by visiting settings, tapping "public mode", and turning on the switch 

note: once in public mode, you will need a passcode to return to private mode.
