[new] this build introduces a new "public mode."

public mode is designed for you to feel safe handing your phone over to a friend for them to browse select pings.

to try it out:
1) go to settings
2) under the "dev settings" heading, tap "try out public mode (experimental)"
3) find a ping older than 24 hours that you'd like to make public
4) tap the "public" button that should now be visible beneath it (or in the long-press menu)
5) enter public mode by visiting settings, tapping "public mode", and turning on the switch (the first time, you'll choose a passcode)

note: once you enter public mode, someone will need your passcode (or your face id) to return to private mode.
