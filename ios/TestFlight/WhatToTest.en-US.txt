3 new features to try... 

#1 Export: when you long-press on a ping, you'll see the option to export it in plain text (along with some of its metadata) 
 
#2 Long-press: notice a new menu appears when you tap and hold on any ping, anywhere in the app. 
 
#3 Custom lenses: "all pings," "re-ping," "afternoons," etc...now you can now create a custom lens. Try searching for a word or phrase. Specifically, something that appears in multiple pings you've captured. Notice an icon "[ + ]" appears next to the search bar. Tap it, and you'll see this lens appear alongside the default ones.
