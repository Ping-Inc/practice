[fixed] the app no longer crashes on launch on iOS 27. if you see anything odd when leaving and returning to the app (backups, face id, sharing, importing), please let us know.

[new] this build introduces a new "public mode."

public mode is designed for you to feel safe handing your phone over to a friend for them to browse select pings. more details below.

[new] turning the dial between focus and grid now zooms in and out, instead of switching instantly.

[new] moving between lenses now gives a light haptic tick, whether you tap a lens or swipe to it.

to try "public mode":
1) go to settings
2) under the "dev settings" heading, tap "try out public mode (experimental)"
3) find a ping older than 24 hours that you'd like to make public
4) tap the "public" button that should now be visible beneath it (or in the long-press menu)
5) enter public mode by visiting settings, tapping "public mode", and turning on the switch (the first time, you'll choose a passcode)

note: once you enter public mode, someone will need your passcode (or your face id) to return to private mode.
