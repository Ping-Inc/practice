[new: public mode] 
this build introduces a new "public mode."

public mode is designed for you to feel safe handing your phone over to a friend for them to browse select pings. more details below.

[new: zoom] 
turning the dial between focus and grid now zooms in and out, instead of switching instantly.

[instructions: public mode]
1) find a ping older than 24 hours that you'd like to make public
2) tap the "public" button that should now be visible beneath it (or in the long-press menu)
3) enter public mode by visiting settings, tapping "public mode", and turning on the switch (the first time, you'll choose a passcode)

note: once in public mode, you will need to enter your passcode (or your face id) to return to private mode.
